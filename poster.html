WEDNESDAY, 18 NOVEMBER 2026
Five stories that defined the day — from the Gulf to Manila.
GULF CRISIS — LEAD STORY

Kuwait Intercepts Drones

Kuwait’s air defences intercept hostile drones in the early hours of Wednesday, hours after US jets hit an Iranian missile site on Qeshm Island. Bahrain, home to the US Fifth Fleet, sounds fresh alerts — then the all-clear.

It is the third Gulf state drawn into the fighting in a single night, after Bahrain and Iran itself. No casualties or damage are reported.

02DIPLOMACY

Trump Meets Marcos in Manila

The US president holds bilateral talks and a working lunch with Ferdinand Marcos Jr. on Wednesday, then flies to Shenzhen for Thursday’s first-ever Xi–Trump–Putin summit. Quad leaders met on Tuesday evening, launching an AI accelerator and critical-minerals deals.

03MARKETS

Dow Sinks 849 Points

The Dow fell 849 points, 1.6%, to 50,759 — down more than 1,000 intraday — and the S&P 500 closed at its lowest since late July, a fourth straight day of losses, as tech shares slid ahead of Nvidia’s earnings.

04MIDDLE EAST

Gaza Truce on the Brink

The US-led Board of Peace warns the Gaza ceasefire faces imminent collapse without action, in a report to the UN Security Council. Fighting continues and the terms of the 2025 deal go unimplemented.

05ASIA

Asian Chip Stocks Rally

Chip stocks jump across Asia on Wednesday after Nvidia’s record $115.8 billion quarter: South Korea’s KOSPI climbs nearly 3%, with SK Hynix up more than 6%, and Japan’s Nikkei up over 1%.

ALSO TODAY

27½ years for Trump murder plot

A US judge sentences Pakistani man Asif Merchant to 330 months in prison for the 2024 murder-for-hire plot against Trump and other politicians, at Iran’s behest.
